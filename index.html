The proof

Before the meme, there was a Doge

Every date below is real history. Look it up.

  1. 697

    The first Doge

    Venice elects its first Doge. The title comes from the Latin dux: leader.

  2. 1284

    The first Doge coin

    Under Doge Giovanni Dandolo, Venice mints the gold ducat: 3.5 g of almost pure gold.

  3. 1500

    The world's money

    The ducat is trusted from London to Cairo. Very trade. Much gold.

  4. 1797

    The last Doge

    Ludovico Manin, the 120th Doge, steps down. The Republic ends after 1,100 years.

  5. 2026

    The return

    2013 gave us a Shiba named Doge. 2026 gives the title back to the original.
